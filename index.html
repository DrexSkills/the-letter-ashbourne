How to play

Your goal

Find out what happened to your father. Your next step is always in the top-left box. Follow the pink arrow. A yellow ! marks who to talk to.

Move

WASD or arrow keys. Hold Shift to run.Drag your thumb on the left side of the screen. Push to the edge to run.

Talk & look

Press E next to people and objects.Tap the E button next to people and objects. Sparkles hide clues and memories.

Fight

Space attacks. Q or 1–4 switches weapon.The red button attacks. ⇄ switches weapon. Dodge the hounds’ lunge and the Warden’s red circle.

Day & night

The Ashen only come out at night. The village and station are always safe, and you heal there. Sleep at June’s café to skip to morning.

Level up

Defeat Ashen to earn XP. Every level gives you an extra heart and stronger hits.

Weapons

Ashen drop ember shards ✦. Greta the blacksmith, south-east of the square, trades them for a sword, a bow and an axe. Dad’s lantern makes every hit stronger.

A pet

A stray dog hides near the bridge, east of the village. Win him over with a meat pie from June’s café and he’ll fight the Ashen with you.

Menus

J journal (story so far), M map, I items, H this help, Esc menu.Buttons in the top-right: map, journal, items, full screen, sound, menu.

Story mode (recommended)

The Letter is a story game. Just want the action? Tap Skip ⏭ on any conversation to jump ahead. Your goal box and the journal’s Story tab keep you on track.

Explore inside

Walk up to a door and press E to go in. The café, bookshop, post office, forge and church all have secrets — and people go indoors at night.

Achievements

21 to unlock — from finishing chapters to bonking every townsperson. See them in the Menu.

See more of the game

Press U or tap Hide to hide the goal box, buttons and minimap. Tap Show UI to bring them back.
