Privacy Policy — The Letter

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The Letter is a single-player story game made by DrexSkills. Your privacy is simple here: the game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your saved game, your settings (such as volume and text speed) and your achievements are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the game's page: github.com/DrexSkills/the-letter-ashbourne.
